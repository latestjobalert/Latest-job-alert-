Sarkari Exam Guides, OTR Rules & Application Tips (2026–27)

1. Railway, SSC & Banking Exam Roadmap (2026–27)

Central recruitments like SSC CHSL, SSC JE, RRB Station Master, RRB Paramedical, and IBPS RRB follow a structured Computer-Based Test (CBT) + Skill/Document Verification cycle. Before applying on ssc.gov.in or indianrailways.gov.in, complete your One-Time Registration (OTR), verify Aadhaar details, and keep your category/EWS certificate updated for the current financial year.

2. Bihar & UP State Exams (BPSC, BSSC, UPSSSC, UPPSC)

State commissions such as BPSC (TRE 4.0), BSSC, UPPSC (RO/ARO), and UPSSSC (PET-based posts) require valid state domicile and non-creamy layer (NCL) certificates for reservation benefits. For UPPSC, OTR registration is mandatory prior to form submission, while Bihar Post Matric Scholarship (pmsonline.bihar.gov.in) requires Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts for DBT.

3. Photo, Signature & PDF Size Rules (Avoid Rejection)

Most form rejections happen due to blurred photos or wrong file sizes. SSC & RRB generally require a 20KB–50KB passport photo and 10KB–20KB black-ink signature on white paper, while educational certificates must be merged into a clear PDF under 200KB–500KB. Many clone websites mimic popular Sarkari portals. Latest Job Alert never asks for bank OTPs or application fees directly — every "Apply Online", "Admit Card", and "Result" button inside our detail cards routes straight to verified .gov.in or .nic.in official portals.
