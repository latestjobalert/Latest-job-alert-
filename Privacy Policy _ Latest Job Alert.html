Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

At Latest Job Alert, accessible from https://latestjobalert.pages.dev, the privacy of our visitors is one of our primary priorities. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and recorded by Latest Job Alert and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact Information: If you contact us directly through our Contact Us form, we may receive your name, email address, and the contents of your message solely to respond to your inquiry.
  • Local Browser Preferences: Features such as saved bookmarks and dark/light theme preferences are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and are not sold to third parties.

2. Log Files

Latest Job Alert follows standard web hosting practices using log files. Information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is used to analyze trends, administer the site, and improve performance.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like most websites, Latest Job Alert uses cookies to store visitor preferences and optimize user experience by customizing web page content based on visitors' browser type or other technical information.

4. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to latestjobalert.pages.dev and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads or managing ad preferences at https://adssettings.google.com/.

5. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Third-party ad servers or ad networks (such as Google AdSense and Adsterra) may use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their advertisements and links that appear on Latest Job Alert. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and personalize advertising content. Latest Job Alert has no access to or control over cookies used by third-party advertisers.

6. Children's Information

Protecting children while using the internet is important to us. Latest Job Alert does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided such information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Consent

You can disable cookies through your individual browser options. By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If we update this policy, changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
